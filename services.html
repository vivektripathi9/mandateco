Services

We Become Your Sales Engine.

GO-DIGITAL provides an outsourced sales organisation for real-estate companies, managing the journey from lead to site visit, negotiation and booking.

01

Lead Generation

Fill the Top of the Funnel.

GO-DIGITAL can handle the entire sales process on behalf of the developer, starting with demand creation across channels that reach the right buyers.

Digital campaigns, portals, referrals and outbound calling work together so the pipeline stays fed with intent.

  • Digital campaigns
  • Portals
  • Referrals
  • Outbound calling

02

Lead Qualification & Inside Sales

Qualify Fast. Nurture With Discipline.

Identify budget, location, property preference and buying timeline before a visit is booked.

Inside sales covers calling, WhatsApp follow-ups and email nurturing so every lead has a clear next step.

  • Budget and location fit
  • Property preference
  • Buying timeline
  • Calling and WhatsApp
  • Email nurturing

03

Site-Visit Management

Schedule and Confirm Property Visits.

Site visits are where interest becomes conviction. We schedule and confirm visits so prepared buyers meet a prepared floor.

We accompany the process with clear ownership before and after the visit so intent does not cool overnight.

  1. Schedule visits
  2. Confirm attendance
  3. Prepare the floor
  4. Post-visit follow-up

04

Field Sales

Turn Interest Into Experience.

The site visit is one of the most important moments in the real estate customer journey. It is where interest becomes conviction - or walks away.

We design the visit as a conversion moment: prepared buyers, a clear product story, and disciplined follow-up after they leave the site.

  • Visit scheduling
  • Customer preparation
  • Site experience
  • Sales presentation
  • Objection handling
  • Post-visit follow-up
  • Conversion

05

CRM Management

Track Every Lead and Stage.

Without a single view of the pipeline, developers see activity - not outcomes. Teams chase noise instead of stuck deals.

We integrate CRM and reporting systems so lead source, stage, owner and conversion are visible from enquiry to closure.

  • Pipeline visibility
  • Stage ownership
  • Source tracking
  • Conversion reporting
  • Weekly performance reviews
  • Actionable dashboards

06

Sales Conversion & Post-Sales

Because the Last Mile Matters.

Sales opportunities can be lost even after strong customer engagement - when negotiation drifts, paperwork stalls, or no one owns the booking.

Our closure-focused approach brings structure to the final stretch so intent becomes a confirmed sale.

  • Negotiation
  • Follow-up
  • Objection handling
  • Commercial discussions
  • Documentation coordination
  • Booking conversion
